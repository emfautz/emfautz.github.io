Healthcare .NET SQL eSignature Offline Sync

Overview

The Assessment & eSignature Platform was a core clinical system used across 230 senior living communities to complete state‑mandated assessments, medication workflows, and compliance documentation. The platform supported configurable assessment logic, automated sub‑assessments, and secure digital signatures — all built on a .NET backend with SQL‑based data storage.

The Problem

Prior to modernization, assessments were completed using paper forms or outdated desktop tools that lacked validation, consistency, and auditability. Staff frequently re‑entered data, made manual corrections, or lost progress due to unreliable connectivity in certain facilities.

  • Paper‑based assessments caused delays and inconsistent documentation
  • No centralized system for tracking assessment completion or compliance
  • Manual entry led to high error rates and rework
  • Facilities with poor Wi‑Fi needed offline support
  • State regulations required secure signatures and audit trails

Approach

I enhanced and extended the .NET application to support configurable assessment workflows, conditional logic, automated sub‑assessments, and secure eSignatures. I also contributed to offline‑capable modules that allowed staff to complete assessments even in low‑connectivity environments.

  • Implemented dynamic assessment logic with conditional branching
  • Built automated sub‑assessments triggered by user responses
  • Added secure eSignature capture with audit‑ready metadata
  • Developed offline‑capable modules with background sync
  • Improved SQL queries and schema for performance and reliability
  • Created alerts and reporting tools for compliance tracking

The Outcome

  • Reduced assessment completion time to **under 5 minutes**
  • Cut manual entry by **90%** through automation and validation
  • Enabled secure, compliant digital signatures across all facilities
  • Supported **230 communities**, including low‑connectivity environments
  • Improved regulatory compliance and reduced documentation errors

What I Learned

This project deepened my experience building systems for regulated environments, designing offline‑capable workflows, and implementing secure digital signatures. It also reinforced the importance of predictable state transitions and user‑friendly clinical tools.
